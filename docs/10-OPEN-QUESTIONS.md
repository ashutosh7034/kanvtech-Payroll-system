# Open Business Questions

These are blockers for legally/financially correct production payroll. Do not fill them with guesses.

1. Which jurisdiction, institution policy set and payroll rules apply to the first production tenant?
2. Which tax financial year is first in scope, and who approves effective-dated tax rules?
3. What are the exact formulas, eligibility, caps and rounding rules for DA, HRA, TA, NPA and other salary heads?
4. What is the exact GPF interest convention (rate source, effective dates, calculation base, credit timing, compounding and rounding)?
5. What day-count rules apply to partial months, leave deductions, arrears and pay fixation?
6. What are the loan interest methods, installment rules, recovery priorities and treatment of insufficient net pay?
7. Which role may generate, verify, finalize and approve; may one person hold multiple roles; is self-approval prohibited?
8. What are the rejection/return paths and who may reopen a returned payroll?
9. What is the required process for correcting approved payroll: reversal, adjustment run, or archive with OTP?
10. What bank file formats, PFMS/CFMS/FSCM interfaces and reconciliation inputs are required?
11. What exact columns and validations are required for each spreadsheet template?
12. Which payslip/report fields are mandatory, optional, masked or institution-specific?
13. What employee/transaction volume, concurrency and performance targets should be tested?
14. What are data retention, document storage, backup frequency, recovery-point and recovery-time objectives?
15. Is multi-institution tenancy required from day one, and may a user have cross-OU assignments?
16. What is the official institution calendar for financial year, payroll periods, increment dates and retirement processing?
17. Which leave types and payability mappings apply, and what system is the authoritative leave source?
18. What exact GPF closure and final-payment approvals are required?
19. Which notifications/email/SMS providers are available, and which messages contain sensitive information?
20. Who signs off each module and the final reconciliation evidence before production use?
